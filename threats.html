Common Online Threats

Explore each card and select "Learn how it works" for more details.

🎣
ONLINE FRAUD

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing confidential information.

Learn how it works +

A scammer may pretend to represent a bank or a familiar company and ask you to click a suspicious link.

Warning sign:
"Your account will be blocked! Verify your details now."

Protection: Verify the sender and use the organisation's official website or app.

🦠
MALICIOUS SOFTWARE

Malware

Harmful software that may damage devices, disrupt activities or steal information.

Learn how it works +

Malware may arrive through unsafe downloads, infected attachments or untrusted applications.

Warning sign:
An unknown website asks you to install an unfamiliar app.

Protection: Download software from trusted sources and keep your device updated.

🔑
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Password Theft

When someone obtains another person's password and attempts to access their account without permission.

Learn how it works +

Reusing passwords across websites can increase risk if one of those services experiences a data breach.

Warning sign:
You receive an unexpected login alert from an unfamiliar device.

Protection: Use unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

💬
ONLINE HARASSMENT

Cyberbullying

Using digital platforms to repeatedly harass, threaten, embarrass or target another person.

Learn how it works +

It may include hurtful messages, abusive comments or sharing someone's private information without permission.

Warning sign:
Someone repeatedly sends threatening or humiliating messages.

Protection: Save relevant evidence, block and report the account, and seek help from a trusted adult.

📨
UNWANTED MESSAGES

Spam

Unwanted messages sent in large numbers, sometimes containing misleading offers or suspicious links.

Learn how it works +

Spam messages may advertise fake offers or try to direct people to untrustworthy websites.

Warning sign:
An unknown sender promises an unbelievable prize.

Protection: Avoid suspicious links, block unwanted senders and report spam.

🪪
PRIVACY RISK

Identity Theft

The unauthorised use of someone's personal information to impersonate them or misuse their identity.

Learn how it works +

Exposed personal details may be misused to impersonate someone or attempt to access their accounts.

Warning sign:
An unfamiliar account is pretending to be you.

Protection: Limit public personal information and report impersonation to the platform.

YOUR FIRST LINE OF DEFENCE

🛡️ Stay One Step Ahead

✓ Use strong, unique passwords.
✓ Never share your OTP or password.
✓ Verify suspicious messages before responding.
✓ Keep your apps and devices updated.
✓ Review privacy settings on your accounts.
✓ Block and report suspicious accounts.
