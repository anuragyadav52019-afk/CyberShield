Cyber Safety Quiz

Test your knowledge of cyber safety, online privacy, strong passwords and responsible internet use. Think carefully before revealing each answer!

🧠 Challenge Your Knowledge

8 questions • Multiple-choice format
Select your answer, then open "Reveal Answer" to check your understanding.

Question 01

What is phishing?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: B. Phishing uses deceptive messages or websites to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

Question 02

Which is the strongest password practice?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: C. Long, unique passwords make accounts harder to compromise and prevent one exposed password from affecting multiple accounts.

Question 03

What should you do if you receive a suspicious link?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: D. Unexpected links can lead to fraudulent websites or harmful downloads. Verify the message through a trusted channel.

Question 04

What does two-factor authentication (2FA) provide?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: A. 2FA requires an additional verification step beyond the password, helping protect an account if its password is exposed.

Question 05

What is cyberbullying?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: B. Cyberbullying involves using digital communication to target, intimidate or humiliate another person. Save relevant evidence and report it to a trusted adult or platform.

Question 06

Why should you install security updates?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: C. Updates often patch known vulnerabilities and improve the security and reliability of devices and applications.

Question 07

Which information should you avoid sharing publicly online?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: D. Passwords and one-time verification codes can allow others to access your accounts. Never disclose them to someone who requests them unexpectedly.

Question 08

What is a healthy digital wellness habit?

🔎 Reveal Answer

Correct answer: A. Regular screen breaks, adequate sleep and time away from devices can support a healthier balance between online and offline life.

💡 Remember: This is a self-checking quiz. The radio buttons let you select an option, and each "Reveal Answer" section lets you check it. Since this project uses HTML and CSS only, it does not calculate your score automatically.
