SMALL HABITS • STRONGER PROTECTION

Make Safety Your Habit.

Protect your digital identity with simple, practical habits. Explore the tips, check your safety habits and take control of your online privacy.

Essential Safety Practices

Select "Read more" on any card to explore practical recommendations.

🔐

1. Secure Your Passwords

Strong, unique passwords help protect your online accounts.

Read more +
  • Use long, unique passwords or passphrases.
  • Avoid obvious personal details.
  • Never reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Consider a trusted password manager.
🛡️

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add another layer of protection to your important accounts.

Read more +

Two-factor authentication requires an additional verification step besides your password.

  • Enable it wherever available.
  • Never share verification codes with others.
  • Protect your account recovery methods.
🔎

3. Identify Suspicious Links

Do not trust every message, link or offer you receive.

Read more +
  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Be cautious of urgent threats and prizes.
  • Verify website addresses before signing in.
  • Visit official websites directly when unsure.
👤

4. Protect Your Privacy

Control what you share and who can see your information.

Read more +
  • Review your social media privacy settings.
  • Avoid publishing your home address or daily routine.
  • Think before posting photos and personal details.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
💻

5. Keep Devices Updated

Updates often include important security improvements.

Read more +
  • Install updates from trusted sources.
  • Remove applications you no longer need.
  • Use built-in security protections.
  • Back up important files regularly.
📶

6. Browse Carefully on Public Wi-Fi

Public networks may not offer the same protection as your trusted home network.

Read more +
  • Confirm the correct network name with the venue.
  • Use secure websites and apps.
  • Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks.
  • Turn off automatic connection to unknown networks.

Think Before You Share

Your digital footprint can last longer than you expect.

✓ Safer Choices

  • Use privacy settings thoughtfully.
  • Share information only when necessary.
  • Ask permission before posting someone else's photo.
  • Check facts before forwarding a message.

✕ Avoid These Habits

  • Posting passwords, OTPs or private documents.
  • Sharing your live location publicly without considering risks.
  • Clicking links in unexpected messages.
  • Forwarding unverified rumours or private content.
